# Tutorial FieldDay
## Overview
The goal of FieldDay is to assist in finding, planning, and playing local pickup sports with people of a similar skill level. By the end of this tutorial, you should have your own FieldDay account customized to your preferences with at least one joined or created session. This workflow should take under 10 minutes.
## Prerequisites
- Have TestFlight downloaded on an iPhone.
- Send an email address to Team 4 so they can invite you.
## Setup / Installation
- Click on link sent to you by Team 4
- Install FieldDay
- Open FieldDay, allowing Location Services
## First Workflow (step-by-step guide)
1. Create an account. You can sign up through Google, or sign up using any email. If you use your own email, choose a strong password that fits the criteria.
2. If you signed up through Google, skip this step. Authenticate your email by entering the 6 digit code that was sent to it.
3. First-time sign up profile customization. Fill in each field as accurately as possible. You need at least a name and one available time slot. You do not have to complete fields marked as optional, although they are recommended.
4. Join a session. You should now be on the home screen. Click the Explore tab at the bottom, and session options will pop up. If you desire to go to a session, click on the session to view details and join it. You can also view sessions by location under the "Map" toggle of the Explore page.
5. (Optional). If you do not see a session you like, click the "Create" tab at the bottom. Fill in session details as you desire. Create the session for the sport, skill level, date, and time you choose. Hopefully you can find a group!
## Expected Results
The joined/created session should appear in the Home page and Chat page. Further specified details by the host may be written in the session chat. The profile tab should display your preferences as chosen at sign up.
## Troubleshooting
- Creating account won't work: ensure that your password fulfills all the requirements. If the 6 digit code didn't come through to your email, try the resend email button and check again.
- Forgot password: click the "Forgot Password" button to be able to enter a new one.
- Profile setup won't complete: make sure you have typed in a name and given at least one time slot you are usually available.
- Location unavailable: turn on Location Services for FieldDay under iPhone settings.
- Can't find a session: broaden filters to allow for more sessions to pop up. There is also always a chance that no one wants to play.
- Can't join a session: it might be full or too late. Choose another session.
