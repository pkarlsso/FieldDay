# Quick Deployment Setup Guide

To quickly install and use FieldDay on your iPhone:

1. Sign in to an Apple Account (Apple ID) on your iPhone, or [create one](https://account.apple.com/) if you do not have one.
2. Download **TestFlight** from the App Store.
3. Send an email to [gabrielogbalor@gmail.com](mailto:gabrielogbalor@gmail.com?subject=Requesting%20Access%20to%20FieldDay%20Testing) with the subject **Requesting Access to FieldDay Testing**. Include the email address associated with your Apple Account.
4. Once the team adds you, you will receive an email inviting you to test FieldDay through TestFlight. Open the invitation and accept it.
5. Download **FieldDay Sports** from TestFlight and enjoy the FieldDay experience. You can create an account with FieldDay or sign in with Google.

If you want to use Google sign-in, please include the Gmail address you would like to use in your request.
