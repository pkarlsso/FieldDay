# Quick Deployment Setup Guide

To quickly install and use FieldDay on your iPhone:

1. Download **TestFlight** from the App Store.
2. Send an email to [gabrielogbalor@gmail.com](mailto:gabrielogbalor@gmail.com?subject=Requesting%20Access%20to%20FieldDay%20Testing) with the subject **Requesting Access to FieldDay Testing**. Include the email address associated with your Apple Account.
3. Once the team adds you, you will receive an email inviting you to test FieldDay through TestFlight. Open the invitation and accept it.
4. Download **FieldDay Sports** from TestFlight and enjoy the FieldDay experience. You can create an account with FieldDay or sign in with Google.

Google sign-in is currently in testing mode, so your Google account may need to be added to the test-user list first. If you want to use Google sign-in, include its email address in your request. If you have trouble receiving the invitation or signing in, reply to the same email for help.
